Giving thanks, upcoming events, and a donation opportunity

Posted Sat, Nov 25, 2023; updated Tue, May 28, 2024

Dear Montague Common Hall Community – we are so grateful for you and the many ways you support and nourish this beautiful old building! As we witness what seems like a world on fire, we are acutely aware of the importance of gathering places where people can learn from each other, eat together, argue our ways toward what is true, sing, dance, and lean-in to all the discomforts and joys of inhabiting this larger Something we are a part of. Thank you.

Solstice in Montague with Lui Collins and Anand Nayak

Friday December 8, 2023, 7:30-9:30pm
The concert features seasonal favorites along with their usual eclectic repertoire of songs, including a taste of Brazilian Bossa nova, originals and standards. All with vocals, guitar, tenor ukulele and banjo. Tickets are $25, and $15 for children under 12, payable at the door by cash or check. Advance reservations to lui@luicollins.net are recommended. If you prefer to pay in advance, you may send payment via Venmo to @luicollins indicating the date and location of the concert you wish to attend. You will receive an email confirmation to present at the door. Vaccination not required; masks requested.

Montague Open Mic

Saturday December 9, 2023, 6:30-9:30pm
All types of performers are encouraged to participate in the Open Mic– musicians, poets, singers, dancers, storytellers. Come hear great music and other performances in a comfortable setting. This Open Mic welcomes and appreciates both emerging musicians and seasoned performers. Performers should sign in before 6:15PM. Time slots may be determined at random depending on how many performers sign up. Come prepared to perform for 10-20 minutes, depending on turnout. Voluntary donations.

Montague Swing Dance

Friday December 15, 2023 7-10pm
Come swing the night away at this monthly event! There will be a dance lesson from 7-8pm, and an open dance with live music provided by the Butterfly Swing Band from 8-10pm. Beginners and experienced dancers are all welcome! Cost is $20 for lesson and dance, or $15 entry for the dance alone.
